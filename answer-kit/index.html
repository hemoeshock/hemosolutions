How the kit answers — three answer shapes

Every entry in the library is written in one of three shapes, so you never have to invent a flattering answer. This table is taken verbatim from the kit:

ShapeWhen to useExample
Yes, with evidenceYou genuinely do this, today, with a record “Yes — MFA is enforced for all workforce accounts. Evidence: IdP policy export, 2026-09-30.”
Not yet, with a dateYou don't, but you have committed to a plan “Not yet. MFA is enforced on all production and billing accounts; the remaining 4 contractor accounts are tracked in access-review-log.csv with a target date of 2026-11-15.”
ScopedYou do it, but only for part of the estate “Yes, for all customer data in production. Staging and CI use synthetic data only and contain no customer data.”

The kit's own rule, printed under that table: never use the third shape to hide the second one.

The triage buckets

BucketMeaningWhat happens next
A — answerable nowYou do this today and can point to evidenceAnswer it now, attach the evidence link
B — needs evidenceYou do this; the record needs to be producedFile the request, name an owner and a date, answer with the commitment
C — needs a decisionSomeone else must decide — legal, founder, a contractEscalate in writing on day one

From the kit: “Most questionnaires are 60–70% bucket A. That is the good news.” The full triage workflow adds how to find the gated rows, how to ask for a standard answer set instead of a row-by-row response, and how to answer the rows that need a decision.

Sample: one real answer

This is an actual entry from src/answer-library/ANSWER-LIBRARY.md (abbreviated):

SQ-A01 · MFA for workforce accounts

Yes — multi-factor authentication is enforced for all workforce accounts that can reach
production or customer data, through the identity provider. Evidence: the IdP policy export
referenced in evidence/access-review-log.csv, re-exported at each quarterly access
review.

Scope: contractor accounts on the legacy billing system are excluded and tracked in
evidence/access-review-log.csv with a target date of 2026-11-15. This answer is deliberately
"scoped" rather than a flat "yes" — see the answer-shapes rule.
